# DOHAY — a wardrobe that remembers

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** DOHAY helps you get dressed with advice grounded in the clothes you actually own.
- **Identity:** Brand-derived warm cream `#F7F4EA`, forest `#3B6454`, and clay `#D09A85`; Playfair Display for editorial emphasis, DM Sans for interface/copy, DM Mono for metadata.
- **Hero:** A conversation that becomes a personal wardrobe, then folds back into a confident outfit decision.
- **Motion:** Camera-led interface tour; garment-photo cards unfold into individual saved pieces; serif headlines slice through clean UI planes.
- **Arc:** An immediate everyday question opens into context-aware styling, then a satisfying wardrobe reveal and a quieter role-specific owner glimpse.
- **Payoff:** Interface cards fold into the real DOHAY logo on an inverted forest field; the logo's round form becomes the opening aperture.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 32 seconds.

## Fully scripted shots

### Shot 01 / 0.00–5.10 s / 5.10 s — “WHAT SHOULD I WEAR?”
**Purpose:** Begin with the everyday uncertainty DOHAY answers, clearly framed as personal styling rather than a shopping product.

**Exact content and timing:** At 0.00 s, a cropped outfit portrait appears as a large circular photo aperture. At 0.25 s “WHAT SHOULD I” punches in at upper-left; at 0.72 s “WEAR?” replaces the second line at 30vmin. At 1.45 s the small line “A stylist who starts with your real closet.” appears. At 2.20 s a phone-chat plane snaps in on the right with “How does my outfit look?” and a photo thumbnail. At 3.10 s “Quick fit check” appears as a noninteractive visual UI chip. Hold through 4.25 s; move into the circular thumbnail from 4.25–5.00 s.

**Composition / artistic idea:** Cream paper field. Hero photo aperture is 43% of frame height, cropped on the right; typography owns the left half. Clay rule grows beneath “WEAR?” while a fine forest orbit line traces the photo edge. Phone glass has warm paper tint, forest text, and a soft grounded shadow.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.22 s aperture pops to 0.96 with stiff spring; 0.48 s subtitle line shears in; 0.72 s “WEAR?” takes over by scale-to-focus. 1.55–2.20 s phone assembles from two offset planes. 2.45 s thumbnail pulses once, creating the lens target. 3.25 s camera punches 1.16× into that circle; question and subtitle shear away as it expands.

**Transition contract:** Circle from the selfie thumbnail becomes the circular mask of Shot 02; one forest message bubble remains visible at its center. Compound transition: circular zoom-through + split cream/forest field.

**Assets/technique:** SVG/interface art built in scene; official `attached_assets/dohay-logo.svg` reserved for finale. Fonts from Google Fonts. No photo claim or fit guarantee.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental bed, warm editorial piano with brushed percussion and a restrained pulse; target a clear accent at 5.0 s. Beat alignment pending generated track.

**Assets/technique:** SVG drawn garment silhouettes as explanatory abstraction (not actual user photos), cards and tracing paths. Small wardrobe photo abstraction never implies a real customer's uploaded image.

### Shot 04 / 16.80–22.65 s / 5.85 s — “AI SUGGESTS. YOU DECIDE.”

**Choreography:** Card arrives through label push. Attribute slips stamp down sequentially and settle. Olive tag briefly flips on its Y axis into forest green; user-edit note resolves. **Transition contract:** Swatch roundel becomes the palette wheel in Shot 05. Compound transition: split-panel unfold + tag flip + circular swatch match cut.

**Assets/technique:** Original/simulation artwork shown as abstract illustrated panels, not actual generated body image; label is legible. No promise of virtual try-on or fit accuracy.

**Sound intent:** Music's 22 s accent is a planned cut point, alignment pending.
